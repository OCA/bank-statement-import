This module pulls the bank statements of German banks directly via FinTS
(formerly HBCI), without any third party service in between.

The transactions are fetched as MT940 statements or, for banks which do
not offer them, as CAMT reports, imported with the CAMT parser of
``account_statement_import_camt``.

Due to PSD2 the bank asks to confirm the access (strong customer
authentication) from time to time, usually every 90 or 180 days, some
banks on every access. The confirmation is done in Odoo with a TAN or,
for decoupled methods, in the banking app. In between, the statements
are pulled automatically.

Configuration
=============

FinTS requires a product registration number, which Die Deutsche
Kreditwirtschaft assigns for free at
https://www.fints.org/de/hersteller/produktregistrierung.

To configure the provider:

1. Go to *Invoicing > Configuration > Journals* and open the bank
   journal, its bank account must be set.
2. Set *Bank Feeds* to *Online* and select *FinTS* as provider.
3. As administrator, open the provider and fill in the bank code (BLZ),
   the FinTS server URL of your bank, your login, your PIN and the FinTS
   product ID.
4. Click on *Connect*. Confirm the access with a TAN or in your banking
   app, as asked by the wizard.

The PIN and the state of the FinTS connection, including the account
numbers and names provided by the bank, are stored unencrypted in the
database. Restrict the access to the database and its backups
accordingly. Only administrators can read and set them; this applies to
the password of the providers of other services too.

The transactions provided via FinTS carry no unique identifier.
Selecting FinTS as provider therefore enables *Generate Missing Import
IDs* of ``account_statement_import_generate_unique_id`` on the journal.
It cannot be disabled while FinTS is the provider of the journal,
otherwise pulling an overlapping period would duplicate the lines.

The available TAN methods are listed after the first connection. To use
another one than the automatically selected method, set its code as *TAN
Method* and connect again.

Check also the configuration of ``account_statement_import_online`` for
the schedule of the pulls.

Some banks ask to confirm every access in their banking app. For them,
set *Wait for App Confirmation* to the minutes a pull waits for the
confirmation, and schedule the pulls when you can confirm them, e.g.
once a day.

While waiting, the pull keeps an Odoo worker busy. With multiple
workers, Odoo stops a scheduled pull after ``limit_time_real_cron``
seconds (by default ``limit_time_real``, 120 seconds) and a manual pull
after ``limit_time_real`` seconds. Keep the waiting time below these
limits or raise them accordingly.

Usage
=====

The statements are pulled by the scheduled action of
``account_statement_import_online``, or manually with *Pull Online Bank
Statement* on the provider.

When the bank asks to confirm the access again, the provider switches to
*Confirmation Required* and its followers get a message. Click on
*Connect* and confirm the access, until then no statements are pulled.

Development
===========

This module implements FinTS only on top of
`python-fints <https://github.com/raphaelm/python-fints>`__. Changes
needed for a specific bank, e.g. to work around a bank that deviates
from the FinTS specification or from the parameters it announces, do not
belong in this module: propose them as a pull request to python-fints
instead, so that every user of the library benefits from them.
